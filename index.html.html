Progression Rules

If every set misses the lower target range, maintain or reduce load 5-10%.
If the lower target is reached but not the top, keep load and add 1 total rep next session.
If all sets hit the top target with RIR >= 2 and good or acceptable form, increase load.
Pain, poor form, high soreness, or low confidence blocks aggressive overload.

How calculations work

RIR estimates how close a set was to failure. Lower RIR generally means higher fatigue and lower short-term readiness.

Estimated recovery uses volume, RIR, soreness, exercise type, muscle role, lower-body status, eccentric emphasis, and unaccustomed work.

Progressive overload is recommended only when performance, recovery, pain status, and form quality are adequate.

HRmax uses 220-age by default. Custom HRmax overrides that formula. Zone 2 is estimated as 60-70% of HRmax.

Weekly aerobic targets follow general cardiovascular prevention guidance: 150 min/week moderate aerobic activity minimum, and 300 min/week for additional benefit.

This app is for personal training guidance and does not replace medical evaluation.
